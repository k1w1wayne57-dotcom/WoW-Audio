# Models still needing specs (please supply)

Models where reliable spec data can't be found online. If you can paste specs
(or a rear-panel photo / tube count), I'll add them. The fields that matter here
are **watts/ch, frequency response, THD, weight, year**. Original ¥ list price and
USD MSRP are nice-to-have and are *not* treated as gaps in this list — most blanks
across the DB are just those two.
